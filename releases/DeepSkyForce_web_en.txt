DeepSkyForce - Highlights for the website
(what is worth highlighting in each published release, in plain language; most recent first)

26/11/2026
- **Finer, sharper stars.** Your images gain definition: stars come out smaller and more faint stars show up, without touching a thing.
- **Watch your image take shape.** While it is processing, you see the steps of the work and the image appearing with each frame added.
- **The Hubble palette, your way.** Choose how much gold and how much blue you want, even zone by zone, and leave the rest of the image in its natural colour.

21/11/2026
- **Striking nebulae without losing detail.** The smart finish brings out the nebula and darkens the sky, and now keeps stars and fine details just as they are: a far more striking image that still looks real.

15/11/2026
- **Ask the app your questions.** Type in your own words how to do something and an assistant answers with what the manual says, with the exact page in case you want to read more.

12/11/2026
- **You decide which frames get stacked.** After the analysis you see every frame with the automatic selection already ticked, and can keep or drop any of them before stacking.

10/11/2026
- **Build on your earlier work.** If you already have a stacked image from a past night, even if you deleted the original frames or did not make it with this app, you can add it to the new session and fill gaps with new shots.

02/11/2026
- **Retouch just part of the image.** With zones you can add colour or boost only the red or the blue of a nebula in a specific area, without touching the rest.
- **A more striking finish, no AI.** A new Hubble palette in gold and cyan, colours without blotches and more detail in the nebula without adding grain.
- **In English and Spanish.** The app and its manual open in your computer's language.
